GEO audit // Generative engine optimization

How AI search engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Gemini) parse AquaVolt

When users prompt AI models with a question, the model does not read intuition. It retrieves structured knowledge-graph entities and trusted citations — and it cites whatever the web actually offers it.

“Who is the most qualified solar geyser installer in Gqeberha with certified electrical sign-off?”

The AI model action

Without dedicated service pages and structured data of its own, AquaVolt has nothing quotable for the model to retrieve — so AI engines fall back to the directory pages that do have structured listings.

The GEO remedy

Deploy clean JSON-LD linking AquaVolt directly to Kwikot ELON specifications and certified sign-off credentials, plus one entity page per service, so AI search can cite the business that does the work.
